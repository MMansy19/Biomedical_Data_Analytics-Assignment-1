**Overview** 

Choose any medical dataset of your choice in CSV format from a trusted source (e.g. Kaggle,  PhysioNet, UCI Repository). Your dataset must contain at least 5 numeric columns and 200+ rows of  real biomedical or clinical data. Apply what you learned in the tutorials to explore, visualize, perform  EDA, clean, filter, and normalize the data. Your notebook should tell a clear data analytics story with  brief explanations before each step. 

Note: You must start your notebook by stating the dataset name, its source URL, the number of rows  and columns, and why you chose it. 

**Part 1 — Data Exploration** 

Explore the dataset and answer: 

\- What is the dataset about and what does each column represent? 

\- What are the data types of each column?  

\- Are there any missing or suspicious values (e.g. impossible zeros)? 

\- What are your conclusions from the basic statistics using df.describe()? 

**Part 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** 

Perform a thorough EDA on your chosen dataset: 

\- Plot the distribution of at least 3 numeric features using histograms. 

\- Create a boxplot for at least 2 features to visually inspect outliers. 

\- Compute and visualize the correlation matrix as a heatmap. Identify the top 2 most correlated  feature pairs and explain what this means clinically. 

\- Plot a scatter plot between any 2 features that show a strong correlation. 

\- State at least 3 observations from your EDA and explain what they tell you about the data. 

**Part 3 — Visualization & Filtering** 

\- Choose at least 2 features and visualize them as line plots with proper labels and grid. \- Simulate a noisy signal on any feature of your choice. 

\- Apply the most appropriate filter to clean the signal and justify why you chose it. \- Plot the original, noisy, and filtered signals together on the same graph. 

**Part 4 — Cleaning & Normalization** 

\- Identify and handle missing or impossible values using the most suitable method. Justify your  choice. 

\- Detect outliers using both IQR and Z-score methods and decide which to remove and why. \- Apply the most appropriate normalization method to at least 3 features. Explain why you  chose it for each. 

\- Show statistics (mean, std, min, max) before and after normalization.
